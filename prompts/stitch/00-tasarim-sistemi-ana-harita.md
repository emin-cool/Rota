# Stitch Prompt 0 — Tasarım sistemi + Ana Harita

**Nasıl kullanılır:** stitch.withgoogle.com → App modu → aşağıdaki kod bloğunun içini kopyala-yapıştır → Generate. Beğenene kadar düzelt; sonraki promptları **aynı projede** ver.
**Dışa aktarınca kaydet:** `design/stitch/home-map.html` + `design/stitch/DESIGN.md`

```text
Design a mobile app (iOS style, 390x844) called "Rota" — a map-first social fitness app for walking, running and cycling routes in Istanbul. All UI text must be in Turkish.

Style: clean, energetic, outdoorsy but premium. Light mode. Primary color deep Bosphorus teal #0E7C66, accent sunset orange #FF6B35, neutral background #F7F8F6, text #111827. Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (headings bold, body regular). Corner radius 16px for cards, fully rounded pill buttons. Soft shadows, generous spacing (8pt grid). Icons: rounded line icons.

Screen: Home Map. Full-screen light map of Istanbul's Bosphorus shoreline. Top: search bar "Nereye gitmek istersin?" with profile avatar. Floating filter chips below it: "Yürüyüş", "Koşu", "Bisiklet". On the map, 4 colored route polylines with small rating badges (e.g. "4.7 ★"). Bottom sheet peeking with title "Yakınındaki popüler rotalar" and a horizontal carousel of route cards (route thumbnail, "Emirgan Sahil Yürüyüşü", "4,8 km · 1 sa · Kolay", "4.7 ★ (312)"). Large floating primary button "Rota Oluştur" with a sparkle-route icon. Bottom tab bar with 5 tabs: Harita, Keşfet, center raised "+" Kaydet button, Akış, Profil.
```

**Düzeltme örnekleri:**
- `Show 3 color scheme variations of this screen`
- `Make the "Rota Oluştur" button larger and move it above the bottom sheet`
- `Use a slightly darker map style with teal water`
